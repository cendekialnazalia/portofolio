## Brand & Style

This design system embraces a **Tactile / Collage Scrapbook** aesthetic layered with punchy Neo-Brutalist structural accents. It translates physical craftsmanship—torn parchment edges, textured coordinate grids, masking tape strips, polaroid cutouts, and hand-annotated margins—into a sharp, dynamic web surface.

The audience consists of creative professionals, design studios, culture-forward clients, and digital craft enthusiasts seeking personality over sterile minimalism. The interface should feel tangible, expressive, and humanly authored rather than mechanically generated.

Key aesthetic drivers:
- **Physical Collage Metaphor**: Surfaces evoke physical sheets of heavy rag paper (#F1F0EC), dark cutting mats, and textured kraft paper with procedural Perlin noise crumpling.
- **Graphic Tension**: High-contrast, dense black ink structures punctuated by an electric red-orange accent (#E8392B).
- **Deliberate Imperfection**: Asymmetrical angles (subtle -4° to +2.5° element tilts), raw torn paper silhouettes, sticky notes, and sketchy handwritten marginalia alongside hyper-structural typographic grids.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic physical substrate balanced with intense typographic ink and focused hot accents:

- **Primary Accent (`#E8392B`)**: An assertive vermilion red-orange applied to primary CTA highlights, interactive outline borders, sticker offset drop-shadows, and live focus rings.
- **Secondary Ink (`#111111`)**: Deep carbon ink used for solid display text, dark structural marquee bands, primary button shells, and hard drop shadows.
- **Tertiary Forest (`#2F6B4C`)**: A self-healing cutting mat tone used for specialty workbench cards, inset panels, and contextual craft containers.
- **Neutral Paper (`#F1F0EC`)**: The foundational tactile canvas simulating heavy, unbleached cotton paper.
- **Ink Soft (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.62)`)**: Muted editorial body text, contextual labels, and metadata.
- **Coordinate Grid (`rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.07)`)**: Subtle linear coordinate graph strokes overlaying the paper substrate.
- **Material Accents**: Post-it yellow (`#FFF6B8`) for testimonial snippets, translucent tape wash (`rgba(225, 222, 214, 0.82)`), and crisp cutout white (`#FFFFFF`) for sticker backing strokes.

## Typography

Typography plays a dual role: structural, aggressive, brutalist titling paired with spontaneous, personal marginalia.

- **Primary Display & Headings (`Archivo Narrow`)**: Utilized in ultra-bold (`800` to `900`) weights with tight line heights and slight negative tracking. Titling mimics poster typesetting and letterpress ink stamps. Headlines default to full uppercase transformations.
- **Handwritten Accent & Marginalia**: Organic notes, directional arrows, author signatures, and callout labels utilize an informal handwritten display face (such as `Caveat`) set in weights `700` at sizes `20px` to `30px`, rendered with subtle rotations (-3° to 5°).
- **Technical Indexing (`Space Mono`)**: Used strictly for structural metadata, status pill tabs, page coordinate indices, revision dates, and file tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The canvas is constructed on a visible 26px linear coordinate grid drawn over the textured paper surface. 

- **Grid Architecture**: Standard desktop layouts utilize a 12-column grid within a maximum container width of `1320px`. The grid supports asymmetric collage arrangements where photo cutouts, sticker badges, and sticky notes break out of standard bounding boxes.
- **Section Rhythm**: Broad vertical pacing (`space-2xl` to `margin-lg`) contrasts with tightly packed interior component cards. Full-bleed black ink bands break up sections with negative top offsets (-26px) to lock against the background grid.
- **Responsive Behavior**:
  - *Desktop (>1000px)*: Multi-column asymmetrical dossier layouts, floating margin sticky notes, rotated photo stickers.
  - *Tablet (761px - 1000px)*: 2-column stacked sheets, unified margins (`margin-md`), adjusted folder panels.
  - *Mobile (≤760px)*: Single-column linear flow, horizontal sticker carousels, sticky notes resting directly inline, and navigation collapsed into high-contrast sticker buttons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, blurry modern dropshadows. Instead, elevation relies on physical collage layering, neo-brutalist hard offsets, and surface textures:

- **Hard Brutalist Offsets**: Interactive elements, badges, and cards use flat, crisp offset shadows without blur:
  - Standard Buttons: `4px 4px 0px #E8392B` (on hover: `6px 6px 0px #E8392B` with a `-2px, -2px` translation).
  - Floating Controls: `3px 3px 0px #111111`.
  - Stacked Dossier Folders: `0 -10px 30px -18px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)` to create dimensional separation between heavy paper layers.
- **Die-Cut Sticker Backings**: Photo cutouts and brand tiles are framed by multi-stage crisp solid strokes (3px white outline backed by a 2px carbon ink offset), emulating vinyl stickers peeled from a backing sheet.
- **Physical Overlays**: Translucent masking tape strips (`rgba(225, 222, 214, 0.82)`) span across card corners at alternating 45° angles.
- **Paper Noise Texture**: Background surfaces integrate a procedural Perlin noise filter (`feTurbulence` with `feDiffuseLighting`) layered via CSS multiply blend modes over `#F1F0EC` to emulate physical crumpled stationery.

## Shapes

The primary shape profile is deliberate, razor-sharp geometric cuts (`roundedness: 0`):

- **Zero-Radius Foundations**: Primary buttons, folder tabs, sticky notes, photo borders, and section bands use strict 90-degree corners.
- **Torn Paper Edges**: Section transitions and photo cutout dividers utilize dynamic polygonal clipping masks (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) with variable irregular jagged teeth (0px to 14px step offsets) to simulate hand-ripped kraft paper.
- **Physical Accents**: Circular elements (`50%` radius) are reserved for stamp badges, rotating graphic starbursts (`✦`, `✺`), and focal camera cutouts. Specialty tool chips use small `8px` to `10px` soft corners to resemble tactile app icon stickers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Crisp rectangular `#111111` fill with `#F1F0EC` uppercase bold typography, framed with a 0px border-radius and a `4px 4px 0px #E8392B` hard drop shadow. Hover shifts element `-2px -2px` with a `6px 6px 0px #E8392B` shadow.
- **Ghost / Outline Button**: `2px solid #111111` border with transparent or `#FFFFFF` fill, uppercase mono or condensed bold text, and a `3px 3px 0px #111111` hard offset shadow.

### Sticky Notes & Dossier Cards
- **Sticky Note**: Pale yellow `#FFF6B8` surface rotated at `-2°` to `+3°`, containing hand-annotated headings, dark ink text, and simulated translucent tape strips applied at the top midpoint.
- **Project Folder**: Layered tabbed folders with a visible file tab labeled in uppercase `Space Mono`. Solid `1.5px solid #111111` structural dividers with subtle folded-corner treatments.

### Photo Cutouts & Polaroids
- Cutout photography framed in heavy 6px `#FFFFFF` borders with high-contrast drop-shadows. Corners are visually affixed to the layout canvas with semi-transparent scotch tape elements (`rgba(225, 222, 214, 0.82)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Sticker Badges**: High-contrast white rounded or pill containers with hard outlines (`1.5px solid #111111`), containing saturated tool icons or category labels, slightly rotated to resemble manually pasted physical stickers.
- **Index Tabs**: Monospaced `#111111` on `#F1F0EC` inverted label tabs (`0.12em` letter spacing) used for indexing project categories and years.

### Input Fields
- Flat `#FFFFFF` or `#F1F0EC` rectangular fields with a sharp `2px solid #111111` border, dark placeholder text in `Archivo Narrow`, and a vibrant `#E8392B` bounding box highlight on focus.
